GridVox — go-to-market (quote-first approach)
=============================================

Strategy in one line
--------------------
Free GitHub repo = trust magnet + DIY filter.
Public page = no sticker prices. Fixed quote after a 15-minute hardware & privacy check.
Internal guidance: solo professional often mid-hundreds; multi-user + documents higher.
Do NOT lead with Gumroad kits. Do NOT sell to LocalLLaMA hobbyists.
Do NOT white-label Open WebUI.

Email for all sales: support@gridvoxsystems.com
Offer page: https://laxmanbyte.github.io/ollama-ultimate-stack/remote-setup.html


Who to contact (yes)
--------------------
- Solo owners / practice managers (law, accounting, clinic, consulting)
- People who say “I need private ChatGPT” or “staff keep pasting into ChatGPT”
- MSPs / bookkeepers / IT people who already have those clients


Who to ignore (no)
------------------
- Reddit LocalLLaMA / HN power users who want free DIY tips
- Anyone asking for white-label branding removal
- “Make me $19 Gumroad pack” as the main business


How to price (internal — never post exact dollars on the public page)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Quote from three inputs: hardware, user count, documents/knowledge base.
- Solo professional, chat-only, decent PC ........ usually mid-hundreds
- Small firm handoff + notes / support ........... higher than solo
- Multi-user + private document search ............ higher still
- Compliance-heavy / air-gap / custom ............. separate quote


30-day execution
----------------
Week 1: Send message A to 20 warm contacts. Send C to 5 MSPs / partners.
Week 2: Book 2–3 hardware checks. Deliver fixed quotes. Close installs.
Week 3: Upsell document knowledge base where files must stay local.
Week 4: Collect 2-sentence testimonials. Keep public page quote-first.
